# Client email — accompanying note per deliverable

After rendering, draft a short covering email that hands over the PDF. Voice (brand
book): **measured · authoritative · warm · precise.** Spain uses **tú**; Latin America
uses **usted**. One tagline per asset at most — usually none in an email. Never attach a
sales pitch to a legal document; match the tone to the family.

**Signature / contact canon (brand book):**
- EN assets → `hello@menteapex.com` · ES assets → `hola@menteapex.com`
- Domain `menteapex.com` only. No ™/® (pre-registration). Sign **Boris Vujicic · Mente Apex**.

**Drafting mechanics:** if the Gmail connector (`mcp__claude_ai_Gmail__*`) is available
in the session, create a **draft** (never send) to the client contact — pull the address
from the client registry / `PROJECT.md`, or ask. If it isn't available, output the
finished subject + body for the user to paste. Attach the rendered PDF path either way.

## Subjects + tone by family

Placeholders: `<Client>`, `<what>`, `<date>`.

| Family | EN subject | Tone in one line |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Initial offer / Proposal | `Your offer — <what>` | Warm, confident, value-first. Invite one dated next step; no pressure. |
| Engagement agreement | `Engagement agreement — <Client> × Mente Apex` | Plain, reassuring. "Here's what we agreed, in writing." |
| IP licence | `Your licence for <what>` | Precise, calm. State what they own; avoid legalese theatre. |
| DPA | `Data-processing agreement — <what>` | Matter-of-fact, compliance-forward. |
| Handover | `<what> — yours: what you own & where it lives` | Generous, clear. Plain-language ownership. |
| Update brief | `<what> — milestone update (<date>)` | Brief, momentum-building; flag anything needing sign-off. |
| Pre-production notice | `Before we go live — <what>` | Careful, opens the opt-out window; note the ~7-day date. |

## Spanish / Croatian subject forms

- **es-ES (tú)** / **es-419 (usted)** — offer/proposal: `Tu propuesta — <what>` (ES) ·
  `Su propuesta — <what>` (419). Agreement: `Acuerdo de servicios — <Client> × Mente Apex`.
- **hr** — offer/proposal: `Vaša ponuda — <what>`. Agreement: `Ugovor o suradnji — <Client> × Mente Apex`.

Keep the body to 3–5 sentences: greet, one line on what's attached and why, the one next
step (or "no action needed — for your records"), sign off. Mirror the deliverable's
language; don't mix.
